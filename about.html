About Last Door Left

Last Door Left is an independent YouTube channel making original animated survival stories. Every episode asks one simple question: if a zombie outbreak started right now, could you survive 30 days where you are?

Each story is set in one ordinary place, such as a hotel room or an office tower. Our hero is an ordinary person with no special skills. Supplies are counted, problems come one at a time, and every survival method in the story is based on real, checked facts.

What we publish

Full episodes

About 10 minutes each, on YouTube.

Daily parts

Short chapters on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts.

Original work

Our own stories, artwork, characters and narration.

The series is made in India for an English-speaking audience. All content is fictional animation for teens and adults; it is not made for children.
