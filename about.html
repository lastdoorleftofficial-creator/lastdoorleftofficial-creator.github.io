About the series

Last Door Left is an independent YouTube channel making original animated survival stories. Every episode asks one question: if a zombie outbreak started right now, could you last 30 days where you are?

Each story is set in one ordinary place, like a hotel room or an office tower. The hero is an ordinary person with no special skills. Supplies are counted, problems arrive one at a time, and every survival method is based on real, checked facts.

Full episodes run about 10 minutes on YouTube. Short daily parts go out on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts. The stories, artwork, characters and narration are our own.

The series is made in India for an English-speaking audience. It is fictional animation for teens and adults, not made for children.
