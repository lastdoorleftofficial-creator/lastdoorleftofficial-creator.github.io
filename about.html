About the series

Last Door Left is an independent YouTube channel making original animated survival stories. Every episode asks one question: if a zombie outbreak started right now, could you last 30 days where you are?

Each story is set in one ordinary place. The hero is an ordinary person with no special skills, the supplies are counted, and every survival method is based on real, checked facts.

Full episodes

About 10 minutes each, on YouTube. One place, 30 days, told day by day with chapters, so you can follow every decision.

Episode 1 is set in a hotel. Episode 2 is set in an office tower.

Daily parts

Each episode is also cut into five short parts for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, one new part every day.

The ending is only in the full episode.

Our own work

The stories, artwork, characters and narration are made for this channel. Our hero is easy to spot: the blue cap never comes off.

Fictional animation for teens and adults. Not made for children.
