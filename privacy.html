Privacy Policy

Last Door Left Uploader · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Last Door Left Uploader ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Last Door Left YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and caption files, schedule them, and add them to the channel's own playlists. It reads only the signed-in channel's name and ID, to confirm it is working on the correct channel.

What the app stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app keeps a local log of its own uploads (video IDs and links). It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about viewers or any other people.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
